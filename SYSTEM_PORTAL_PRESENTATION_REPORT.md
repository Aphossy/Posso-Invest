# 10/10 Ventures Portal

## Team Presentation Report

**Prepared:** September 2026  
**Product:** 10/10 Ventures savings, investment, and governance portal  
**Primary users:** Members, Secretary, Treasurer, President, Admin, and Advisors  
**Operating context:** Kigali, Rwanda  
**Tagline:** Save together. Build together.

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The 10/10 Ventures portal is the group’s digital operating system. It centralizes savings, loans, meetings, communications, documents, records, and governance in one secure, role-based platform.

This is not just a website. It is the system that gives the group visibility, accountability, and structure across every major activity. Members can track their own savings and requests. Leaders can coordinate financial and governance work. Administrators can manage the platform and members with a clear operational view.

The portal supports the group’s operational model and helps the business move from informal coordination to a disciplined, traceable operating framework. It creates a single place where the team can:

- record contributions and manage dues
- process loan requests and repayments
- track attendance, meetings, and action items
- share announcements and messages
- keep documents, reports, and assets organized
- monitor the overall group health and progress

The result is a more transparent, efficient, and accountable organization.

## 3. Why the Portal Matters

The group is operating with a serious financial and governance model. Without a central system, this creates friction and risk:

1. Members do not always know their full financial status.
2. Officers have to collect updates manually across tools and conversations.
3. Meeting decisions and follow-ups are difficult to track consistently.
4. Financial records and supporting documents can become fragmented.
5. Management loses time on reporting, reconciliation, and follow-up.

This portal addresses those issues by making the work visible and structured. It reduces manual work, supports accountability, and creates a cleaner record of the organization’s operations.

## 6. End-to-End Team Workflows

### Workflow A: Monthly contribution cycle
1. The Treasurer opens the contribution window.
2. Members see the active payment period and due dates.
3. Contributions are recorded with supporting details.
4. The system marks them as pending, confirmed, late, or waived.
5. Penalties are assigned where applicable.
6. The dashboard updates collection status and outstanding totals.
7. Leaders review the financial picture and follow up where needed.

### Workflow B: Loan process
1. A member submits a loan request.
2. The request is reviewed against the group’s rules.
3. The President or committee reviews and approves or rejects it.
4. Treasury records disbursement and repayment tracking.
5. The system monitors loan status, balances, and due dates.
6. Members and leadership receive status updates and notifications.

### Workflow C: Governance cycle
1. The Secretary schedules a meeting.
2. Attendance is recorded.
3. Minutes and resolutions are captured.
4. Action items are assigned to owners.
5. The dashboard tracks completion and upcoming accountability.
6. Members can review the decisions and outcomes.

### Workflow D: Reporting and audit preparation
1. The Treasurer prepares the financial period.
2. The system collects outstanding contributions, loans, penalties, and expenses.
3. Audit and report views summarize the state of the organization.
4. Leadership reviews the information and resolves exceptions.
5. Reports can be shared and exported as needed.

### Workflow E: New member onboarding
1. Admin or authorized user issues an invitation.
2. The invited member accepts and creates an account.
3. Their role and organization access are assigned.
4. They complete profile and payout details.
5. They access the portal and begin using the relevant member features.

## 7. How the Portal Works: Step-by-Step Operating Procedure

The portal works like a digital operating system for the group. It follows a simple logic: user signs in, role is identified, data is assigned to the organization, then the system directs the person to the tools relevant to their responsibility.

### 7.1 Login and onboarding
1. A member or leader is invited or registered in the system.
2. They sign in using a secure authentication flow.
3. Their session is validated by the platform.
4. The system identifies their organization and assigned role.
5. They are redirected to the correct dashboard and menus for that role.

This means each person sees only the workflows they are allowed to use. A member does not see the treasury panel, and an admin does not see only a member dashboard.

### 7.2 Role-based access system
The portal uses role-based access to separate responsibilities:

- Member: personal financial data, meetings, documents, announcements, profile
- Secretary: governance records, meetings, attendance, minutes, action items
- Treasurer: contributions, payments, penalties, receipts, loans, reports, audits
- President: oversight, approvals, governance issues, financial health, leadership actions
- Admin: platform configuration, member management, system oversight, organization controls

This role separation is crucial because the system is not only a record keeper; it is also a control system that ensures the right people manage the right responsibilities.

### 7.3 Contribution workflow
The contribution process is one of the core operational flows:

1. The Treasurer opens the contribution window for the month.
2. The contribution period becomes active in the portal.
3. Members view their due amount, payment due date, and status.
4. Members pay into the group according to the agreed contribution rule.
5. The Treasurer records the payment and marks the contribution as confirmed or pending.
6. If a member pays late, the system can trigger a penalty record.
7. Receipts are issued and archived for accountability.
8. The dashboards update the collection rate, outstanding amounts, and financial health.

This process ensures that everyone can see whether the group is meeting its savings target and who is compliant or overdue.

### 7.4 Loan workflow
The loan flow is designed to be transparent and controlled:

1. A member opens the loan request form.
2. The member enters amount, purpose, and repayment details.
3. The request is sent to the leadership or treasury review workflow.
4. The system checks if the member is eligible under the group rules.
5. The President or committee reviews the request.
6. The request is approved, rejected, or returned for correction.
7. If approved, Treasury processes disbursement.
8. Repayment tracking begins and the system monitors due dates and outstanding balances.
9. The member and relevant leaders receive notifications as the loan moves through its life cycle.

This creates a clear and traceable borrowing process with accountability at each stage.

### 7.5 Meeting and governance workflow
The governance process works as follows:

1. The Secretary schedules a meeting with agenda and logistics.
2. Members are informed and attendance is tracked.
3. The meeting takes place and attendance is recorded.
4. Minutes and decisions are captured in the system.
5. Any follow-up tasks are converted into action items.
6. Each action item is assigned to a person and due date.
7. The dashboard tracks open, completed, blocked, or overdue tasks.
8. Members and leadership can review the official record of what happened.

This ensures that decisions are not lost in conversation or memory. They become part of the institutional record.

### 7.6 Expense workflow
Operational expenses follow a review structure similar to other governance processes:

1. A member or officer submits an expense request.
2. Supporting information or documents are attached.
3. The request is reviewed by the relevant role.
4. It is approved, rejected, or pending more information.
5. The approved expense is recorded in the system.
6. The financial summaries and reports reflect the updated cost position.

This makes the group’s spending visible and accountable instead of informal or untracked.

### 7.7 Communication and announcement system
Communication is managed within the portal through:

- group announcements for all members
- role-specific updates and notices
- direct messages or support requests
- email notifications for membership and operational events
- reminders for payment windows, deadlines, and approvals

This helps the team stay aligned without relying only on WhatsApp, phone calls, or spreadsheets.

### 7.8 Report and audit workflow
The portal turns operational data into management information:

1. Financial and governance records are stored in the system.
2. The Treasury and leadership review contribution totals, loans, penalties, and expensess.
3. The system generates summaries, dashboards, and audit-ready snapshots.
4. Leaders view outstanding issues, performance, and risk areas.
5. Reports can be refreshed and exported for decisions or review.

This makes governance and financial oversight much easier than checking records manually.

### 7.9 Admin and platform control workflow
The admin role manages the operational system itself:

- member records and organization membership
- role assignments and access control
- platform health monitoring
- message handling and document management
- asset and library management
- operational oversight of the whole organization

The platform is therefore not just a front-end for users; it is also a platform administration environment that supports the order and discipline of the group.

### 7.10 How the data flows in the system
The core data flow is simple:

- Users log in and are assigned roles.
- Their actions are stored against their organization and their own account.
- Financial actions change contribution, loan, expense, or fund records.
- Governance actions create meetings, minutes, decisions, and action items.
- Reports and dashboards read from those records and summarize the current status.
- Notifications and emails trigger when important events happen.

In other words, the system is designed to keep all the information connected so it can support decisions, tracking, accountability, and visibility.

## 8. Business Value to the Team

The portal creates value in four major areas:

### 1. Operational clarity
The team no longer needs to rely on fragmented spreadsheets, inboxes, and verbal updates. The system turns recurring work into a structured digital process.

### 2. Accountability
Each action, decision, contribution, loan, and message has a clear owner and status. This is essential for a growing organization.

### 3. Trust and transparency
Members can see the information relevant to them, while leadership has the data needed for oversight and decision-making.

### 4. Scalability
As the group grows, the platform can support more members, more decisions, more reports, and more complex financial management without losing structure.

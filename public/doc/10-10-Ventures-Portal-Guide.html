10/10 Ventures01 · At a glance

One workspace, different responsibilities

Your portal at a glance

10/10 Ventures uses the portal to make member records, group communications, governance and financial tracking easier to find and maintain.

What you can do

Stay informed

Read announcements, upcoming activities, meeting details and shared documents.

Follow your records

Review your contribution history, receipts, loan requests, attendance and assigned actions.

Coordinate group work

Authorized officers record contributions, maintain meeting records, publish notices and track follow-ups.

Review the bigger picture

Committee dashboards and reports summarize group activity for users with the relevant access.

Role-based access

Your role determines the pages and actions shown in your workspace. Common roles include:

Member

Personal savings, contributions, loans, meetings, documents and group notices.

Treasurer

Contribution and loan records, receipts, expenses, financial files and reports.

Secretary

Schedules, attendance, minutes, action items, announcements and group documents.

President

Governance, member standing, group health and financial oversight.

Administrator

Platform and organization management, member records and broad oversight.

Advisor

Uses the member-facing workspace, subject to the access assigned to the account.

Not seeing a page? It may not be part of your assigned role, or the group may not have enabled that workflow. Contact an authorized officer rather than using another person's account.

10/10 Ventures02 · Access

Your first visit

Signing in and getting started

Portal access is intended for invited or approved users. Use the email address associated with your account and keep your sign-in details private.

Accept an invitation

  1. Find the invitation email. Check your inbox and Spam/Junk folder. Confirm which email address the invitation was sent to.
  2. Sign in with that account. Use the sign-in option offered on the portal. If you choose Google, use the same email address as the invitation.
  3. Return to the invitation. Open the accept-invitation link from your email and follow the on-screen instructions.
  4. Confirm your workspace. After access is granted, you are routed to the workspace associated with your role.

Returning to the portal

Open 1010ventures.online and choose the portal sign-in link, or use the sign-in link supplied by the group. Depending on account setup, sign-in may use email and password or Google. Some accounts may be asked for a six-digit email verification code as an additional sign-in step.

Forgot your password?

Choose the password-recovery link on the sign-in page and follow the email instructions. Use the newest recovery link and do not forward it to anyone.

Account not verified?

Follow the verification prompt to request a new verification email. If it does not arrive, check Spam/Junk and confirm the email address is correct.

Invitation trouble? Ask the administrator or officer who manages access to check the invitation and email address. Do not create a second account or share a sign-in with another member.

First-minute checklist

□ Confirm your name and email in the account menu.

□ Review your profile and account settings.

□ Open your dashboard and read recent announcements.

□ Save the portal address as a bookmark on your own device.

A familiar starting point

Finding your way around

Your dashboard is the home base for the role assigned to you. Use the labeled sidebar to move between tasks; the available sections differ by role.

AreaWhat to look for
DashboardA summary of relevant records, recent activity and links to common tasks.
SidebarGrouped links for your role, such as Contributions, Meetings, Reports or Documents.

NotificationsAlerts and shortcuts for items that may need your attention.

Useful navigation habits

  • Check the page heading before entering or editing a record.
  • Use the sidebar labels rather than guessing a web address.
  • When a list has filters, confirm the selected period or status before drawing conclusions.
  • Use Refresh if the page offers it and you expect a recently updated record.
  • Sign out when you finish, especially on a shared or public device.

Role changes. The portal displays menus based on the role assigned to your account. If you believe your access is incorrect, ask an administrator to review it; do not use a colleague's credentials.

Personal settings

Use your profile page to review the personal information available to you. Account settings may include preferences and security controls. If a detail is locked or incorrect, ask the officer responsible for member records to update it.

10/10 Ventures04 · Contributions

Member workspace

Savings and contributions

Use your member pages to follow contribution periods, check recorded payments and find available receipts. A portal record is useful for tracking, but always retain the proof of payment provided by your bank or payment service.

Review your contribution record

  1. Open My Contributions or My Savings from the member navigation.
  2. Check the period, amount, date recorded and current status for each entry.
  3. Open a record to see its details. Where a receipt is available, check its number and download or save it for your records.
  4. Compare the entry with your own payment confirmation. If something does not match, contact the treasurer with the period, amount and proof.

Understand the labels

ConfirmedPendingLateWaived

A status describes how a contribution is recorded in the portal; it does not replace the group's approved rules. A pending entry may still need officer review. Ask the treasurer if the status or amount is unclear.

Contribution Calendar

Review the periods and payment-window information shown to members.

Group Bank Accounts

Check the official account details before making a transfer. Follow the latest instructions from the group.

Contribution History

Review past entries, status labels and receipt information in one place.

My Savings

See your savings summary where records are available for your account.

Payment safety. Verify the destination account and any reference instructions through an official group channel before sending money. Never rely on a changed account detail sent in an unexpected message.

10/10 Ventures05 · Loans

Member workspace

Loan requests

The member loan page lets you submit a request and follow its status. Decisions, rates, eligibility and repayment conditions are governed by current group policy and the authorized review process.

Submit carefully

  1. Open Loans from your member menu and choose the request action.
  2. Enter the requested amount and any required term or supporting details. Check every field before submitting.
  3. Review the summary and submit once. Keep a note of the request details for reference.
  4. Return to your loan list to follow the status and open a request for details.

Track progress

Your loan list may show statuses such as requested, approved, disbursed, repaying, overdue, repaid or rejected. A status is a record of the workflow—not a guarantee that funds have been or will be transferred. Check the request details and contact the reviewing officer if you need clarification.

Requested

The request has been submitted and may be awaiting review.

Approved / disbursed

Review the approved amount and terms; confirm transfer details with the treasurer.

Repaying

Use the repayment details and history shown in the record to track progress.

Overdue / repaid / rejected

Read the record and follow up with the responsible officer if the outcome needs explanation.

Before you submit. Confirm the requested amount, repayment term and any displayed estimates. The portal's displayed figures are based on the saved record; ask the treasurer to confirm the approved terms.

While a request is still in the requested state, the page may offer options to edit or delete it. Once the request moves to another status, those options may no longer be available. Avoid submitting duplicate requests if the page appears slow; refresh and check your list first.

10/10 Ventures06 · Share-out & penalties

Member workspace

Share-out and penalties

These sections help members review the records used for year-end distribution and any penalty entries associated with their account.

My Share-out

Open the share-out page to review the distribution information available to you. If a period, amount or status is missing, it may not yet have been prepared or published.

  • Confirm the period and figures shown on your record.
  • Read any accompanying explanation or committee notice.
  • Ask the treasurer about calculations or payment status.
  • Ask the president or committee about an approved policy decision.

My Penalties

Use the penalties page to review entries recorded against your account. The page may display status or waiver information; the official group policy determines how a penalty is applied.

  • Check the period, reason and amount shown.
  • Compare the entry with the relevant notice or attendance/payment record.
  • Contact the treasurer promptly if you believe a record is incorrect.
  • Keep any response or evidence with your own records.

Don't infer a rule from a screen. Eligibility, due dates, penalty rates, waivers and distribution calculations come from approved group decisions—not from this guide. Refer to the constitution and current committee communication.

Useful supporting pages

Constitution & Policies

Read the governance documents available to members.

Announcements

Check for current instructions and dates published by the group.

Group Bank Accounts

Verify official account information using the portal and trusted channels.

Messages / Support

Send a question through the help option available in your workspace.

10/10 Ventures08 · Treasurer

Staff workspace

Treasurer workspace

The treasurer's workspace centralizes financial record-keeping. Use the portal to maintain a clear record trail and follow the group's approved controls for handling funds.

Common workflow

  1. Set the contribution window. Review the period, dates and published instructions before opening or closing a window.
  2. Record contributions. Match the member, period and amount to the available payment evidence before saving.
  3. Issue receipts. Confirm the contribution record and receipt details, then make the receipt available to the member.
  4. Review exceptions. Check late or penalty records, and apply changes only where authorized under group policy.
  5. Follow loans and expenses. Review requests, disbursements, repayments and expense records using the appropriate pages.
  6. Reconcile and report. Compare portal records to source statements and keep supporting financial documents together.

Contribution Window

Control the payment period and its member-facing information.

Member Contributions / Receipts

Record and verify payments, then issue or find receipts.

Loan Requests / Repayments

Review the workflow and track payout and repayment records.

Expenses / Penalties

Track operational expenses and penalty entries under approved procedures.

Good control practice. Verify the source document, member, amount and period before changing a record. Follow the group's approval and separation-of-duties rules; a portal action does not itself authorize a transaction.

10/10 Ventures09 · Secretary

Staff workspace

Secretary workspace

The secretary workspace supports the group's meeting rhythm and shared records, from publishing a schedule to documenting decisions and tracking follow-up.

Before, during and after a meeting

Before

Add or update the meeting schedule and planned activities. Confirm the date, time, venue and member-facing details before publishing.

During

Use the attendance register to record who attended. Capture notes and decisions in line with the group's record-keeping practice.

After

Publish minutes, create or update action items, assign owners and record agreed follow-up information.

Between meetings

Share announcements, respond to member correspondence and maintain the document library and formal letters.

Keep records useful

  • Use clear titles and dates so members can find the correct meeting or file.
  • Separate confirmed decisions from discussion notes and proposed actions.
  • Check action owners and follow-up details before publishing minutes.
  • Correct attendance or meeting details promptly when reliable information is provided.
  • Store documents in the appropriate library and respect access and privacy expectations.

Coordination tip. The treasurer may need approved minutes or decisions to support financial records. Share the official record through the portal's appropriate document area rather than relying on an informal message alone.

10/10 Ventures11 · Administrator

Staff workspace

Administrator workspace

Administrator access covers platform and organization-level tasks. Because these pages can affect member access and official records, use them only for authorized work.

Key areas

Members

Maintain member records and handle access-related tasks available to administrators.

Organization & Leadership

Review organization information and committee-term records.

Financial Oversight

Review contributions, loans, penalties, share-out and audit information.

Legal, Banking & Files

Maintain relevant registration information, bank-account details and shared records.

Communication & Governance

Coordinate messages, announcements, attendance and action items.

Platform Health

Review dashboard and health information and report technical problems to the appropriate support contact.

Before changing a member or organization record

  1. Confirm the request came from an authorized source and applies to the correct person or organization.
  2. Review the current record before editing; check identifiers to avoid changing the wrong entry.
  3. Make only the change required and confirm that the saved details are correct.
  4. Where appropriate, record the reason through the group's established process and notify the affected officer.

Protect access. Grant, change or remove access only under the group's approval process. Never share administrator credentials or leave an administrator session open on a shared device.

Reference & good practice

Reports, records & getting help

Financial reports help authorized officers review activity over time. Use them alongside source records and approved decisions.

Reports available to financial staff

Report / areaTypical use
Fund TrackerReview income, expenses and the displayed net balance.
Monthly SummaryReview recorded income and outflows for a period.

Check the period, filters and data freshness. Reconcile totals to statements, receipts and portal entries; send discrepancies and supporting evidence to the treasurer or administrator.

When something goes wrong

Can't sign in: confirm your email, reset your password and complete any verification step.

Invitation missing: check Spam/Junk; ask the issuing officer to verify the address.

Page won't load: check your connection and retry. Note the page and time if it persists.

Record looks incorrect: don't duplicate or alter it; send details to the responsible officer.

Need help: use Support or Messages if available, or contact an authorized officer.

Keep your account safe

  • Never share your password, sign-in link or one-time verification code.
  • Use a unique password and sign out from shared devices.
  • Be cautious with unexpected requests to change bank details or transfer funds; verify through a trusted channel.
  • Share member and financial information only with people who need it for group work.
